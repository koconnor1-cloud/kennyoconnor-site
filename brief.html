The Format

One Idea. Every Week.

Each edition is designed to be substantive enough to matter, but concise enough to read in a few minutes. The goal is not to summarize the news—it is to explain why an issue matters and what it means for business.

Why The O’Connor Brief?

The O’Connor Brief brings together academic research, executive experience, classroom experimentation, and an interest in the forces changing business. Expect perspectives on emerging technology, resilient supply chains, sales, transportation, business education, leadership, defense manufacturing, and the changing value of human capability.

Full editions live here on KennyOConnor.com. Selected ideas will also be adapted for LinkedIn so the conversation can continue beyond the website.

Short. Useful. Evidence-Based.

New issues of The O’Connor Brief will be published weekly, typically in the 700–1,000 word range with a short source list for readers who want to dig deeper.
