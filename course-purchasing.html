Supplier thinking

Move beyond price alone. Consider capability, quality, service, risk, capacity, fit, and total value.

Preparation

Good sourcing and negotiation depend on information gathered before the conversation begins.

Total value

Analyze total cost of ownership and operational consequences, not only quoted unit price.

Judgment

Balance measurable cost with harder-to-quantify operational, relationship, and risk tradeoffs.
