AI for Business
Students

Use AI to extend your thinking and practice—not to outsource the judgment, communication, and credibility that make you valuable.

Use AI as a thinking partner

AI is most valuable when it helps you generate alternatives, pressure-test ideas, explain difficult concepts, or practice applying what you are learning.

  • Ask AI to challenge your assumptions, not only agree with you.
  • Use it to generate practice questions or role-play scenarios.
  • Ask for multiple approaches and compare them.
  • Explain your reasoning back in your own words.

Verify before you trust

Generative AI can produce confident answers that are incomplete or wrong. Treat verification as part of the workflow.

  • Check factual claims against credible sources.
  • Confirm calculations and citations independently.
  • Do not submit invented sources, data, or quotations.
  • Protect confidential, proprietary, and personally sensitive information.

Use AI to practice career skills

AI can help you rehearse interviews, refine résumé bullets, compare a job posting with your experience, and prepare for networking conversations.

  • Provide truthful context about your own experience.
  • Ask for critique before asking for a rewrite.
  • Keep the final language natural and recognizably yours.
  • Never fabricate accomplishments or skills.

Protect the skills that create human value

As routine tasks become easier to automate, communication, judgment, persuasion, creativity, relationship-building, and accountability become more important—not less.

  • Practice difficult conversations.
  • Make decisions with incomplete information.
  • Learn to explain tradeoffs clearly.
  • Build domain knowledge so you can evaluate AI output.
