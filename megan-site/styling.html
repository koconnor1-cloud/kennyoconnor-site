MEGAN’S POINT OF VIEW

Personal style should look like a person.

A strong outfit does not have to be loud, expensive, or trend-driven. Megan pays attention to the details that make clothing work together: silhouette, balance, color, texture, layering, shoes, accessories, and the way the client wants to feel.

PersonalYour taste, comfort, lifestyle, and personality lead the process.
DistinctiveSmall unexpected combinations can make familiar pieces feel new.
WearableThe goal is not a photo-ready closet you cannot use—it is real outfits for real life.
IntentionalEvery addition should have a reason and work with the wardrobe around it.

HOW IT WORKS

From “I have nothing to wear” to a wardrobe with possibilities.

Megan starts by understanding your style, your routines, the settings you dress for, and the pieces you naturally reach for. Then she builds from there.

Understand your style

Talk through what you love, what you avoid, where you go, and how you want to feel in your clothes.

See what you already have

Identify strong pieces, hidden combinations, wardrobe gaps, and items that no longer serve the client.

Build complete looks

Combine clothing, shoes, layers, and accessories into intentional ensembles rather than isolated pieces.

Make style repeatable

Create enough logic and variety that you can continue building outfits confidently on your own.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect.

Do I need a whole new wardrobe?

No. A major part of personal styling is seeing what is already there differently. New purchases should solve specific gaps, not replace a wardrobe by default.

Can Megan style clothing I already own?

Yes. Closet-based styling can uncover combinations, layers, accessories, and uses that are easy to miss when you see the same pieces every day.

Can styling be focused on one special event?

Yes. A single-event session can focus on a complete ensemble and can also coordinate with Megan’s makeup artistry when appropriate.

Is the goal to follow current fashion trends?

Trends can be useful inspiration, but the client comes first. The goal is a distinctive look that suits the person, occasion, comfort level, and lifestyle.
