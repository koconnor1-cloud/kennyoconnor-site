MEGAN’S APPROACH

Enhance. Refine. Never disguise.

The best makeup does more than follow a trend. Megan considers the entire look—your features, skin, clothing, setting, lighting, personality, and the way you want to feel when you walk into the room.

  • Looks tailored to your features and preferences
  • Natural, soft-glam, and more elevated finishes
  • Coordination with wardrobe and event styling
  • Attention to the full look, not just individual products

Define the look

Talk through the occasion, outfit, inspiration, comfort level, and the overall mood you want.

Create with intention

Build a balanced look with attention to complexion, eyes, color, shape, and finish.

Refine the details

Finish the look so it feels cohesive, photographs well, and still feels unmistakably like you.
