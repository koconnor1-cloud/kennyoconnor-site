MEGAN’S APPROACH

Enhance. Refine. Never disguise.

The strongest makeup look is one that supports the person wearing it. Megan’s approach can range from subtle to elevated, but the through-line is the same: flattering choices, careful balance, and a result that feels intentional rather than overdone.

PersonalizedThe look starts with your face, preferences, and comfort level.
CohesiveColor and finish are considered in the context of wardrobe and occasion.

THE PROCESS

A beautiful result starts before the first brush.

A short conversation about the event, wardrobe, inspiration, and desired finish helps define the direction before the look is created.

Define the occasion

Consider where you are going, what you are wearing, the lighting, photos, and how polished or dramatic you want to feel.

Choose the direction

Translate inspiration into a look that makes sense for your features, skin, colors, and comfort level.

Build the look

Create balance across complexion, eyes, brows, cheeks, lips, and finish rather than treating each area separately.

Refine the details

Make final adjustments so the look feels cohesive in person and photographs beautifully.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What to expect.

Can I request a natural makeup look?

Yes. Personalized artistry means the level of makeup should match your preferences. A polished result does not have to mean a dramatic one.

Can Megan coordinate makeup with my outfit?

Yes. Color, neckline, accessories, event setting, and overall style can all influence the makeup direction, especially when styling and makeup services are combined.

Can I bring inspiration photos?

Absolutely. Inspiration can help communicate the mood you like. Megan can then interpret that direction in a way that suits your own features and coloring.

Is this page final regarding exact services?

Not yet. This is the published mockup. Before public launch we will confirm Megan’s exact service menu, policies, timing, products, travel/service area, and booking details.
