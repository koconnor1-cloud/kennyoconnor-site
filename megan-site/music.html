Megan’s teaching philosophy centers on the student in front of her. Lessons are designed to build a strong musical foundation while keeping encouragement, creativity, and genuine enjoyment at the center of the experience.

A PERSONAL WAY TO LEARN

Progress that feels encouraging.

Music instruction works best when technical growth and personal connection reinforce each other. Megan’s goal is to help students understand what they are learning, experience real progress, and build confidence along the way.

FOUNDATION

Musical Skills

Build technique, musical understanding, and reliable habits through clear instruction and purposeful practice.

CONFIDENCE

Comfort Performing

Develop the confidence to play, practice, make mistakes, recover, and keep moving forward without fear of getting everything perfect.

INDEPENDENCE

Better Practice

Learn how to approach difficult passages, break skills into manageable pieces, and make practice time more productive.

CREATIVITY

Enjoyment & Expression

Keep the joy of making music visible so learning is not reduced to checking boxes or simply finishing assignments.

LESSON PHILOSOPHY

Teach the student, not just the lesson.

Students arrive with different experiences, motivations, attention styles, confidence levels, and goals. Megan’s approach is to meet the learner where they are, give them structure, and create enough momentum that improvement feels possible and rewarding.

EncouragingHigh expectations can coexist with warmth, patience, and perspective.
IndividualizedThe pace and emphasis should reflect the learner, not a rigid template.
StructuredClear goals and focused practice help students recognize their own progress.
CreativeTechnique matters, but connection to the music matters too.

HOW LESSONS GROW

Build the foundation. Then build momentum.

A strong lesson experience gives students something specific to work on, a way to understand improvement, and a reason to stay connected to the music.

Start with the student

Understand prior experience, musical interests, current strengths, confidence, and what the student hopes to accomplish.

Set meaningful goals

Create manageable targets that make progress visible rather than overwhelming.

Practice with purpose

Develop a clearer way to work through challenges instead of simply repeating the same passage.

Celebrate growth

Recognize improvement and build the confidence to keep reaching for the next level.

FINDING THE RIGHT FIT

Lesson details should match what Megan actually teaches.

BEGINNING

Building Fundamentals

A supportive starting point for learners who need the basic building blocks, clear routines, and confidence with a new musical skill.

  • Foundational technique
  • Practice habits

  • Confidence-building progress
CONTINUING

Growing Musicians

For students who already have experience and want more consistency, stronger technique, and continued musical growth.

  • Skill refinement
  • Musical interpretation
  • Goal-based practice
  • Ongoing feedback
CUSTOM

Individual Goals

A more focused approach when a learner has a particular piece, performance, skill, or musical goal in mind.

  • Goal discussion
  • Focused instruction
  • Practice strategy
  • Progress checkpoints
