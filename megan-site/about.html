THE COMMON THREAD

Different talents. One point of view.

At first glance, organizing a closet, styling an outfit, creating a makeup look, and teaching music may seem unrelated. For Megan, they come from the same place: listening closely, noticing what others miss, and helping people bring more intention to the way they live and express themselves.

Her approach is warm, individualized, and practical. She is not interested in forcing someone into a preset aesthetic. She wants the end result to fit the person—and to feel natural enough that they can carry it forward themselves.
