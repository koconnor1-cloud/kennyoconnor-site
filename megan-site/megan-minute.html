WHY “A MINUTE”?

Because good ideas do not have to take all afternoon.

The format should feel light, useful, and distinctly Megan. Some posts may literally take a minute to read. Others can be visual tips, mini before-and-afters, quick videos, checklists, or short personal notes. The name gives the content flexibility without making it feel disposable.

QuickDesigned for ideas someone can absorb or use immediately.
VisualPerfect for photos, short videos, transformations, and demonstrations.
UsefulEvery post should leave the reader with something practical.
PersonalThe voice should sound like Megan—not generic lifestyle copy.
